# Unhurried

A phone-line demo that doesn't cut off callers who stammer. Built for the Hiya Voice AI Challenge, Accessibility track.

## Users

- Reviewers watching a 2–3 minute demo video.
- People who stammer.

The page must feel calm and never rushed.

## The one screen

- "Call Northwind Energy" button
- Baseline / Unhurried toggle
- "Why it's waiting" panel: latest decision and its reason, plus the last END or PROMPT
- Raw and clean transcript, side by side
- Bot lines
- Always-visible keypad
- Caller choice: sample caller or my microphone

## Priority

The toggle and the "Why it's waiting" panel are the most prominent things on the page. The video is about them.

## Mode

Operate: task-focused product UI, not a marketing page.

## Design rules

- WCAG AA contrast minimum, visible focus states, keypad fully usable by keyboard.
- Respects reduced motion. No countdown timers, no flashing.
- No gradients, glassmorphism or decorative animation. Calm and clear.
- Readable on a laptop screen recording.
